---
title:  Bartificer Creations Style Guide
---

All articles, show notes, and other media produced under the *Bartificer Creations* brand should adhere to the following writing conventions.

In terms of spelling, vocabulary, and grammar, content should be written in Hiberno-English.

* **passkey** (not *Passkey*) — they term has become genericised like *password*
* __*spidey-sense*__ (italicised)
* **synchronisable** (not *synchronizable*, which is US English)
* **vice versa** (un-italicised, no hyphen)
